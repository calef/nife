# Navigation and naming: the appendices to milestone 47

[`design/roadmap/0047-navigation-and-naming.md`](../0047-navigation-and-naming.md) carries where
the block stands, the model in brief, the reframe, the builtins, the earned divergences, the
finding that drove the build order, and the Follow-on. This directory carries each section's full
text, which the page's prose budget has no room for. Every section here was kept as history beside
a note saying what got built, and the appendices keep that shape.

- [`status-history.md`](status-history.md): the block's status record, from the stale
  `IN-PROGRESS` token through each piece as it landed and the corrections the status checks found.
- [`mkdir-and-rm.md`](mkdir-and-rm.md): `mkdir` and `rm`, and "`rmdir` and `rm -r`: Unix already
  made the safe choice".
- [`links-and-bind.md`](links-and-bind.md): `ln`, the settled fork that chose `bind` over stored
  paths with its naming search, and where `rm` meets links.
- [`touch-and-globbing.md`](touch-and-globbing.md): both halves of `touch`, and globbing with the
  set grant.
- [`absolute-paths-and-bind.md`](absolute-paths-and-bind.md): absolute paths, `bind` and its second
  grant, and why milestone 64 forced the namespace half.
- [`environment.md`](environment.md): the three thirds of the environment, the measured encodings
  and the §111 page.
- [`environment-built.md`](environment-built.md): the config page, `printenv` and real init wiring,
  and the secrets third.
- [`path-and-completion.md`](path-and-completion.md): `PATH`, its sizing and the activation set,
  and completion.
- [`shell-grammar.md`](shell-grammar.md): `file:` and `run` removed, and the function-call syntax
  fork.
