# The register of measures: the appendices to milestone 134

[Milestone 134 (the register of measures)](../0134-the-measurements-that-decide.md) carries the
status, why the register has two tiers and the decisions it serves. It has a table of every measure
with where it stands, and the correction it makes to §95 (an IPC fastpath) and §96 (process kernel
or event kernel). It also has a dated summary of what was built, the BUGS and the radon evening
still owed. This directory carries the detail its prose budget has no room for.

- [`measures.md`](measures.md): each measure's full definition, E1 to E4 and M5 to M12, with its
  mechanism, prediction, instrument and what each outcome settles.
- [`build-record.md`](build-record.md): the dated build narrative from 2026-08-18 to 2026-09-19,
  every result in full, and the corrections and findings made on the way.
