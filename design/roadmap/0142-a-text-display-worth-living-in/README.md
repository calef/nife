# A text display worth living in: the appendices to milestone 142

[Milestone 142 (a text display good enough that people use it instead of a
GUI)](../0142-a-text-display-worth-living-in.md) carries the status of each increment, the
recommendations and what they rest on, the sequencing, what is left for an architect, the `BUGS`
and the follow-on. This directory carries the working its prose budget has no room for.

- [`scanout.md`](scanout.md): the scanout arithmetic as first written, and how increments one and
  two grew the surface to 1280x720 and then retargeted it to 924x344 for a 132x43 grid.
- [`type.md`](type.md): Menlo's provenance and metrics, the three shippable candidates and their
  licenses, and the fork §104 (the rich-text font is DejaVu Sans Mono, and the palette is
  Solarized) settled.
- [`palette.md`](palette.md): Solarized Dark Higher Contrast's origin, how it differs from
  canonical Solarized, and both palettes against milestone 141 (a palette worth looking at)'s three
  properties.
- [`rich-text.md`](rich-text.md): what the cell had to grow, the "bold is bright" reversal, and
  what increment five built on 2026-09-26.
- [`determinism.md`](determinism.md): the two cross-architecture measurements, the cross-version
  finding, the `fontdue` traps, and the rendering choices they force.
- [`dependency.md`](dependency.md): §46 (thin primitives or whole subsystems)'s test, the measured
  dependency graphs, the license facts and the prior art.
